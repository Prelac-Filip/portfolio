## Who we are

Pirate King is a game for Android phones made by Filip Prelac (Green Forest Games), "we" in this policy. You can reach us at [prelacfilip@gmail.com](mailto:prelacfilip@gmail.com).

We run no servers for the game. We never ask for your name, your email address or an account, and the game sends us no data about you. What leaves your phone goes to Google: mostly to Google AdMob, for the ads, and to Google Play, for purchases, plus the time check and Android's backup described below.

## What the game keeps

- **The save:** your progress (your harbor, ships, crew, gold and diamonds, and the purchases already paid out) is kept in a save file on your phone. The game sends it nowhere.
- **Android's backup:** if backup is turned on for your phone, Android may back up the save file to your Google account, so a reinstall on the same account can get it back. That backup is Google's, under Google's privacy policy, and we can't see it.
- **Uninstalling** the game, or clearing its data in Android's settings, deletes the save from your phone.
- **The time:** to keep the game's timers fair, the game asks Google's servers for the current time. That request carries nothing about you or your game, but like any connection it shows your IP address to Google.
- **Settings:** your music and sound volumes are kept on your phone.

## Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows rewarded ads only when you choose to watch one (for free gold, to end a ship's rest early, or to double a chest). It shows no other ads.

The ads come from Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, AdMob may collect and use:

- your phone's **advertising ID** and other device identifiers;
- your **IP address**, from which it works out your approximate location;
- **app interactions**, such as which ads were shown and tapped;
- **diagnostics** about how the ads and the app performed.

This data is sent encrypted. Where the law requires your consent (the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the ads are personalised (chosen using this data) only if you agree: Google's consent form asks for your choice before the first ad, and you can change it at any time with **Privacy choices** in the game's settings (the button shows where the choice applies). Elsewhere, the ads may be personalised. Wherever you are, you can reset or delete your phone's advertising ID in Android's settings (see **Your choices** below).

How Google uses this data:

- [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)

## Purchases (Google Play)

Google Play handles every purchase. We never see your card or payment details. Google tells the game which product was bought, with a purchase code (Google calls it a token), so the game can pay it out. The game keeps that code in the save on your phone, so a purchase is never paid out twice. Google's privacy policy covers the payment itself.

## Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and its audience on Google Play is 13 and over. If you think a child under 13 has used the game, contact us at [prelacfilip@gmail.com](mailto:prelacfilip@gmail.com).

## Your choices

- **Privacy choices** in the game's settings changes your ads consent, where it applies.
- You can reset or delete your phone's advertising ID in Android's settings. Where it is depends on the phone: look for **Ads** under **Privacy** or under **Google**.
- Uninstalling the game, or clearing its data in Android's settings, deletes the save from your phone.
- We hold no data about you, apart from emails you send us. To see or delete those, write to [prelacfilip@gmail.com](mailto:prelacfilip@gmail.com). For the data AdMob and Google Play hold, Google's privacy policy explains how to see it or have it deleted. In the EU you can also complain to your data protection authority.
