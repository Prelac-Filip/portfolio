## Overview

Mostly a static website, with a few SSR pages for guests to enter their desired plans and details, which are then emailed to the client. **Nuxt.js** was chosen because of a planned backend for the site in the future when the client will offer live seminars, a cart functionality, etc..

## What I did

**Everything** from understanding the client needs and wishes, the design and layout of the website, the infrastructure to be used, and the coding. 

Because of the before mentioned *future* need of a backend, I decided to go with the **Nuxt.js** framework because I already worked with **Vue** in my full-time employment at MMK (Booking Manager), and I wanted to try its full-stack solution. Of course, I knew it would come in handy to know it for any upcoming proffessional full-time jobs, as well as give me further insight in the power of Vue.

For the UI elements, to ensure a **fast development**, while still keeping a **proffessional look and feel**, I decided to use **Nuxt UI**. Being very intuitive, a part of the native Nuxt framework, and having a similar usage to **PrimeVue** for customization, it was a great choice.

For **deployment**, I used **Netlify** for a couple of reasons:

- ease of use with Nuxt

- fast deployments of fixes

- zero hosting cost (currently)

- new technology to learn

I have also helped the client to setup the DNS settings, as well as his custom email domain.

## Results

The project was delivered on-time, the client was satisfied, the website is live, and I have learned a lot.

## Stack

Built with Nuxt.js, Vue.js, Nuxt UI.
